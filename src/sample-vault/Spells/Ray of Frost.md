---
type: spell
name: Ray of Frost
level: 0
school: Evocation
casting_time: 1 action
range: 60 feet
components: [V, S]
duration: Instantaneous
classes: [Sorcerer, Wizard]
---

A frigid beam streaks toward a creature. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Cold damage and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

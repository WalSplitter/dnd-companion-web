---
type: spell
name: Mage Armor
level: 1
school: Abjuration
casting_time: 1 action
range: Touch
components: [V, S, M]
duration: 8 hours
classes: [Sorcerer, Wizard]
---

You touch a willing creature who isn't wearing armor. Until the spell ends, the target's base AC becomes 13 plus its Dexterity modifier.

---
type: spell
name: Fireball
level: 3
school: Evocation
casting_time: 1 action
range: 150 feet
components: [V, S, M]
duration: Instantaneous
classes: [Sorcerer, Wizard]
---

A bright streak flashes to a point you choose and blossoms into a roaring fire. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

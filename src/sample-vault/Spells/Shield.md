---
type: spell
name: Shield
level: 1
school: Abjuration
casting_time: 1 reaction
range: Self
components: [V, S]
duration: 1 round
classes: [Sorcerer, Wizard]
---

An invisible barrier of magical force protects you. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +5 bonus to AC and take no damage from Magic Missile.
